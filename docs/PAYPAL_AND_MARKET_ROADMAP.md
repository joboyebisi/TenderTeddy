# PayPal integration and market roadmap

## Checkout

The demo prepares an Orders v2 payload server-side from a trusted SKU/price. Production creates and captures orders only on the server, uses idempotency keys, validates currency/amount/reference before capture, and processes signed webhooks. The recipient may confirm an address during PayPal approval or in WanderWrap's private claim step; fulfilment rules decide which address is authoritative.

Group gifts keep contributions and targets in integer minor currency units. Do not represent collected funds as escrow unless a regulated provider explicitly supports that product in the launch country.

## Launch order

1. UK self-gifting and friend gifting: questionnaires, reminders, real catalogue links, claim links and PayPal sandbox checkout.
2. US/Canada localization: currency, tax, fulfilment and consumer terms per market.
3. Group/corporate gifting: organizer approvals, contribution ledger, bulk recipient import and supplier quotes.
4. Hyperlocal discovery: location permission at the moment of use; merchant availability, distance and stock confidence.
5. Chat agents and broader social connectors only after platform review and measurable consent controls.

## Deck synthesis

The decks consistently support personalization, reminders, recipient claim, physical fulfilment, tracking, individual/corporate gifting, seller commissions and premium business plans. Crypto wallets/CCTP are retained as an optional future payment rail, not a December-launch dependency. Unvalidated growth projections and template contact details are excluded from the operating plan.

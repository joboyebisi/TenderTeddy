# WanderWrap product direction

## Wedge
Christmas 2026 voice-first gift discovery: ask Alexa, answer three questions, receive explainable ideas in the WanderWrap app, then approve a retailer handoff. The shareable gift link creates the Calendly-style growth loop.

## Recommendation model
GiftHub mapped the single highest IBM Watson Big Five trait to five hard-coded eBay keywords. WanderWrap instead uses an optional fine-tuned XLM-R service to propose gift-relevant interest evidence, a separate open-weight Big Five model for private self-discovery, catalogue/vector search for retrieval, and an explainable multi-factor ranker for the decision. User-stated and recipient-confirmed preferences remain strongest; no XLM-R clue becomes rankable before subject confirmation, and an assessed person must separately approve any capped gifting-style signal. Every match exposes its evidence and can be corrected.

## Expansion sequence
1. Conversational self/friend recommendations and gift link.
2. Alexa skill with account linking and app approval.
3. X Christmas campaign only after X grants written AI-reply approval; respond only to explicit mentions/DMs, one response per interaction, with opt-out.
4. Provider-approved social connectors.
5. Hyperlocal merchants and approximate-location nudges.
6. User-controlled computer context and booking adapters, each with per-source consent, preview and final confirmation.

## Safety boundaries
No covert scraping, sensitive-trait inference, continuous location tracking, unsolicited replies, autonomous checkout or bookings. “Read my computer” becomes explicit folder/app grants with a visible activity log, local-first extraction where possible, deletion and revocation.

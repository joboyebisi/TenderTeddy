# TenderTeddy product direction

## Story
Children already speak to Blippi, Alphablocks, Numberblocks and Ms Rachel on screen, but those characters cannot hear them. TenderTeddy makes parent-approved educational media responsive through an original guide, Pye. Named shows are examples parents may already use; they are not bundled content or implied partnerships.

## Family journey
Parent account → add child profiles → choose privacy/voice settings → paste an embeddable or licensed YouTube link → choose learning objectives → preview checkpoints → send lesson to TV → child completes short voice/remote interactions → parent sees skill progress and controls next steps.

## Interaction design principles
- One question at a time with 5–8 seconds of thinking space.
- Acknowledge effort without saying an incorrect answer is correct.
- First miss: scaffolded verbal hint; second miss: model the skill together.
- Gradual difficulty, not random challenge jumps.
- Voice transcript is shown and correctable; remote/touch alternative always available.
- Points reward practice and cooperation, not excessive screen time.
- Parent invite links never expose child details.

## Literacy screening
The `screenLiteracy` adapter supports phonological awareness, letter–sound knowledge, decoding, fluency and comprehension. It selects focus areas and a practice band; it is not a diagnosis. Before integrating the Kaggle dataset, verify its licence, consent/provenance, target label, age distribution, missingness and bias. Map real columns through a versioned importer rather than coupling the product to guessed names.

# Content and Gemini plan

## Launch-safe catalogue

The first catalogue has three explicit modes:

1. **Owned/original TenderTeddy lessons** — full Pye interaction, mid-video checkpoints and reusable clips.
2. **Licensed partner content** — interaction depth follows the signed licence.
3. **YouTube discovery** — parent selects an official video; TenderTeddy checks embeddability and Made-for-Kids status, preserves the YouTube player/branding/controls/ads, and puts learning prompts before or after playback. If playback is unavailable, open the official YouTube URL.

Alphablocks, Numberblocks, Blippi and Ms Rachel are target partnership/catalogue sources, not bundled assets. Their names and media must not appear as if endorsed or licensed until contracts exist.

## Gemini demo

Gemini can analyze an owned/licensed upload or a public YouTube URL to propose timestamped learning moments. The free tier currently exists for eligible models and the video guide states a daily YouTube-video allowance, but free-tier content may be used to improve Google's products. Therefore:

- demo only with public or synthetic/owned media;
- never send child voice, face, profile or private viewing history on the free tier;
- store model/version/prompt and source rights metadata;
- require educator approval before a checkpoint reaches children;
- treat output as candidate curriculum, not an accuracy guarantee.

Production uses a paid configuration with appropriate data terms or a separately approved provider.

PRIVACY NOTICE

Your gifting context stays under your control.

Last updated 22 October 2026.

What we use

WanderWrap uses account details, recipient preferences you confirm, gift decisions, and fulfilment state to provide the gifting service. Optional social, location, and provider connections are off until you enable them.

How we use it

We use confirmed information to rank gift ideas, remember your choices, create private recipient claims, and prepare user-approved provider handoffs. We do not diagnose personality, sell personal data, or purchase or transfer money automatically.

Alexa+ and providers

Alexa+ receives the tool data needed to conduct the conversation and render the experience. Amazon, PayPal, Xoom, or another approved supplier receives information only when required for the route you choose. Payment confirmation remains on the secure provider or Alexa+ checkout surface.

Your choices

You can revoke connected sources, correct recipient preferences, or request account deletion. Recipient delivery details are private and are not shown to the sender.

Contact

Email privacy@wanderwrap.co.uk for access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions.
